Welcome

Welcome to Greater Noida Institute of Technology (GNIOT). The institute provides quality education in engineering, management and computer applications with modern laboratories, experienced faculty members and excellent placement support.

About the Institute

Greater Noida Institute of Technology (GNIOT) is one of the leading educational institutions in Greater Noida. The institute focuses on quality education, innovation, research and overall personality development of students.

Affiliation

Affiliated to Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University (AKTU).

Academic Objectives

  • Provide quality technical education.
  • Develop research and innovation skills.
  • Promote industry-oriented learning.
  • Enhance employability of students.

Facilities

  • Smart Classrooms
  • Digital Library
  • Computer Laboratories
  • Sports Complex
  • Hostel Facilities

Educational Mission

To produce skilled engineers and professionals with strong technical knowledge, leadership qualities and ethical values.
